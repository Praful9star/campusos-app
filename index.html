Why it exists

Stop juggling ten apps for one campus.

Right now it's scattered WhatsApp groups, spreadsheets, the ERP portal, and marketplace DMs with strangers. CampusOS puts it in one app where everyone is a verified @srmu.ac.in student — so a seller is a real junior down the hall, an event RSVP knows who's coming, and notes are tagged to your actual course.

Marketplace

Buy & sell with verified students. Smart, typo-tolerant search, sort, condition & price filters, saved items.

Clubs & Events

Club pages, announcements, and event listings with one-tap RSVP and live registration counts.

Notes

Share course notes, download for offline, filter by course code, report bad uploads.

Chat

Real-time messaging with sellers and classmates, straight from any listing.

Lost & Found

Report and claim lost items across campus, with contact built right in.

AI Assistant

Ask campus questions and get answers drawn from clubs, events, and FAQs.

Get started

Install it in four steps.

  1. Download the app with the button at the top (Android phone or tablet).
  2. Open the file. Android will ask to allow installs from this source — allow it (normal for any app outside the Play Store).
  3. Sign up with your @srmu.ac.in email and a password.
  4. Verify with the code sent to your inbox, and you're in.
Note: this is a free pilot deployment. The server sleeps when idle, so the very first request after a while takes about a minute — just wait or retry once, and it's fast after that.
